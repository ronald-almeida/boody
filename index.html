Is this a physical product?

No. Body Bloom is a digital PDF collection. No physical cards or printed materials are mailed to you.

How do I receive my files?

This is a digital product. Please follow the delivery instructions displayed by the checkout provider after purchase.

What sizes can I print?

Each kit is designed for both US Letter (8.5 × 11 inches) and A4 paper. Print at home or at a local print shop.

Do I need to print everything?

No. Print only the cards or worksheets you want to use. You can also read the PDFs on a phone, tablet, or computer.

No. This is a creative self-care and reflection product, not a weight-loss program, medical treatment, or promise of physical transformation. The rituals are symbolic activities.
